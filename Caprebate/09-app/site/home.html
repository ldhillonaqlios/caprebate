You paid for more than bricks and mortar

Part of what you spent on buying, building or fitting out your premises went on wiring, heating, lifts, fire alarms and lighting. The tax rules let you claim relief on those items, once each one has been found and valued.

View A1 · Bought(01)

You bought premises

Part of the price you paid can be for fixtures that qualify. Whether you can claim depends on things like what the seller claimed and the dates in the sale.

View A2 · Built(02)

You built or extended

The systems you put in can qualify: heating, lifts, lighting, fire alarms and the wiring behind them.

View A3 · Fitted out(03)

You fitted out a lease

You don't have to own the building. If you lease your premises and paid for the fit-out yourself, the items you paid for may qualify.

Questions about capital allowances

Something not covered here? Talk to a person about your building.

Talk to a person ↘
Note 01What are capital allowances?

They are tax relief on money a business spends on qualifying assets. In a building, that includes fixtures such as electrical systems, heating, lifts and fire alarms. The relief lowers the tax the business pays.

Note 02Can I claim on a building I bought?

Often, yes. Part of the price you paid can be for qualifying fixtures. Whether you can claim depends on things like what the seller claimed and dates in the sale. The free assessment asks about these.

Note 03Do I have to own the building?

Not always. If you lease your premises and paid for the fit-out yourself, the items you paid for may qualify.

Note 04What does it cost?

Our fee is a flat 3% of the capital allowances we identify. The free assessment costs nothing.

Note 05Will you work with my accountant?

Yes. Your accountant files your tax return. We give them the figures and the evidence behind them.

Note 06What if HMRC checks the claim?

We support our valuation and cost split free of charge, with no time limit. This does not cover representation at a tax tribunal.
