Capital allowances for healthcare property

Capital allowances for healthcare property: There is tax relief built into your building.

Look inside a clinic

The systems inside healthcare property can qualify for capital allowances, a UK tax relief. Watch where they are.

Most of it is out of sight

It sits behind walls, under floors and above ceilings. A chartered surveyor values the qualifying items and signs every split of costs.

WiringQualifies

Power circuits, distribution boards and the cable runs to every consulting and treatment room.

Heating and hot waterQualifies

The boiler, the hot water cylinder, radiators, pipework and the water to every clinical sink and basin.

Air and medical gasesQualifies

The air handling unit, ductwork and cooling for the treatment rooms, and the compressed air and suction piped to the dental chair.

LiftsQualifies

The lift car, its motor and controls, and the fittings in the shaft, so every floor is step-free.

Fire alarms and patient callQualifies

Detectors, call points, the call buttons in the clinical rooms and the pull cord in the accessible toilet.

LightingQualifies

Light fittings in rooms and corridors, and the emergency lighting on the escape routes.

Questions

Questions about capital allowances.

Something not covered here? Talk to a person about your building.

What are capital allowances?

They are tax relief on money a business spends on qualifying assets. In a building, that includes fixtures such as electrical systems, heating, lifts and fire alarms. The relief lowers the tax the business pays.

Can I claim on a building I bought?

Often, yes. Part of the price you paid can be for qualifying fixtures. Whether you can claim depends on things like what the seller claimed and dates in the sale. The free assessment asks about these.

Do I have to own the building?

Not always. If you lease your premises and paid for the fit-out yourself, the items you paid for may qualify.

What does it cost?

Our fee is a flat 3% of the capital allowances we identify. The free assessment costs nothing.

Will you work with my accountant?

Yes. Your accountant files your tax return. We give them the figures and the evidence behind them.

What if HMRC checks the claim?
