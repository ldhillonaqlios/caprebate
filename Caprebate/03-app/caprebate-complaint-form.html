Make a complaint about how we have handled your personal data

CAP REBATE LTD, trading as Cap Rebate, company number 17307313.

If you think we have got something wrong about your personal data, you can complain to us. This is a right you have under section 164A of the Data Protection Act 2018, not a favour we are doing you.

You do not have to use this form. A complaint by email, by letter, or in a phone call counts just the same, and we will treat it the same way. The form is here to make it easier, not to be a hurdle.

We will confirm we have received your complaint within 30 days — and in practice within a couple of working days. We do not charge for any of this.

This is the printable version. Print this page, fill it in, and post or email it to the address at the bottom. There is also a form you can fill in on screen at https://caprebate.co.uk/notices/.
